# Smart Clipboard 0.4.0 preview — build 14

Turn a captured picture into SVG on your Mac without an AI account or model download. This preview also adds five interface languages and optional capture translation. Configure once, capture, wait for **Clip ✓**, then paste. App windows open only when you ask.

## New in this preview

- **Trace pictures to SVG on your Mac.** Choose **SVG → Trace on device** in General settings. The included VTracer helper follows the visible shapes and colours without uploading your capture. No API key, Python, server, model download or additional setup is needed.
- **Choose the result you need.** Pick **Photo**, **Logo** or **Line drawing**, then **Balanced** or **Detailed**. Detailed keeps more shapes and colours and produces larger files. Your choices are saved for future captures. **Reconstruct with AI** remains the default SVG method, including after upgrading, and uses your selected AI connection.
- **Reuse and export earlier captures.** In History, choose SVG, a method and settings, then **Trace to SVG** or **Convert with AI**. Local traces remain saved by preset and detail alongside AI versions. **Copy** puts SVG source text on the clipboard; **Save…** creates an `.svg` file for a vector editor. Large SVGs show a compact result summary while keeping the full result available to copy or save.
- **Use the app in five languages.** Menus, settings, notifications and accessibility labels support English, Italian, Spanish, French and German. They follow your Mac’s preferred supported language, with English as the fallback. Restart Smart Clipboard after changing its language in macOS.
- **Keep the source language, or translate automatically.** New installations preserve the language detected in an image. To translate AI captures, choose **Settings → General → Languages → Capture output → System language** or a specific language. Changing the interface language does not translate captures. Existing saved directions retain their earlier behavior until you select a language; an explicit language choice takes priority over conflicting translation directions.
- **Translate a saved capture.** In History, choose an **Output language**, then **Convert with AI**. Each format and language keeps its own version under **Saved formats**, alongside local traces.

This preview combines the language features developed in build 12 and local tracing developed and tested in build 13. Neither build was publicly released. Users updating from public preview build 11 receive these changes together in build 14.

## Try local tracing

1. Install the DMG in **Applications**, then open Smart Clipboard. Look for **Clip** in the menu bar.
2. Open **Settings → General**. Set **Preferred format** to **SVG**, choose **Trace on device**, and start with a matching preset and **Balanced** detail.
3. Close Settings and focus the app where you want to paste. Press **⌥⇧⌘3** for an area or **⌥⇧⌘4** for a window; saved custom shortcuts take precedence.
4. Wait for **Clip ✓** or your ready notification, then paste with **⌘V**. The clipboard contains SVG source text. To open it as artwork, use **History → Open → Save…** and import the `.svg` file into your vector editor.

**Clip …** shows work in progress. Failed or cancelled tracing leaves the previous clipboard contents in place. Ready and failure notifications follow your existing preferences; cancellation stays quiet. Opening History does not copy anything automatically: use **Copy**, or enable **Copy after manual conversion**.

Your connection, shortcuts and preferences are retained. Local tracing does not start oMLX or switch your AI provider. To receive preview updates, choose **About → Releases → Stable and preview releases**, then check for updates.

## Which local model should I use?

**For tracing pictures, no model is needed.** Use **Trace on device**.

For AI extraction and translation, start with **mlx-community/Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct-4bit** in oMLX, then select its listed ID using **Refresh models**. The tested server is the official **oMLX 0.7.0.dev2** preview, which fixes the structured-output failure found in 0.6.4. Newer versions are not covered by that evidence.

8B preserved synthetic Spanish text and translated it into English and French in a small test. Other languages and images are not all verified. **32B** needs substantially more memory and did not fix invented descriptions or inaccurate AI SVG reconstruction. See the [model guide](https://colombod.github.io/smart-clipboard/guide.html#choose-a-local-model) for downloads, memory guidance and limitations.

## Preview limitations

- **Tracing is an approximation of visible shapes.** It keeps the background, turns words into outlines rather than editable text, and ignores translation and additional directions. Automatic background removal and accurate chart-data reconstruction are not included. Very large or complex captures may exceed processing limits; try Balanced detail or a smaller area.
- **SVG is copied as source text.** Support for pasting that text as artwork depends on the destination app; saving and importing an `.svg` file is the reliable path for vector editors. SVG uses a validated static subset and is not rendered or executed inside Smart Clipboard. Validation does not prove visual fidelity.
- **AI reconstruction and translation can make mistakes.** Description and SVG fidelity remain limited with the tested local models. Local tracing is a separate method and does not fix their AI output. Review translated text and extracted values; source-language descriptions use English when there is no readable text to identify a language.
- **Pass-through and on-device OCR keep their existing roles.** Pass-through copies the original image without AI. On-device text extraction keeps the source language and does not translate.
- **Cloud connections are not all live-verified.** Account/model access and the real capture workflow still need validation for each route. See [provider status](https://github.com/colombod/smart-clipboard/blob/main/docs/PROVIDERS.md).
- **Full accessibility acceptance remains incomplete.** Localized labels and native controls do not establish complete VoiceOver, pointer-free selection, text enlargement or alternative-input support. See [accessibility status](https://github.com/colombod/smart-clipboard/blob/main/docs/ACCESSIBILITY.md).
- **macOS controls notification delivery and screen access.** Focus or screen-sharing privacy settings can hide or silence alerts. The Clip menu keeps its status indicator. Replacing an older development build can require renewed Screen Recording approval; see [troubleshooting](https://colombod.github.io/smart-clipboard/guide.html#if-capture-does-not-work).

## Verification status

Build 14 has **245 passing tests and five live/render opt-in skips, from 250 total**, with the bundled tracer enabled. Skipped checks are not passes. Required macOS CI passed for [PR #6](https://github.com/colombod/smart-clipboard/pull/6). Apple accepted notarization of the app and DMG; Gatekeeper and exact archive comparisons passed.

An actual Sparkle update from the private build 13 installed the exact build-14 bundle and preserved all 15 existing capture originals and saved results. Installed History showed distinct **Photo / Balanced** and **Photo / Detailed** labels and loaded the matching settings. Native **Save…** exports matched their stored SVGs exactly, at 905,115 and 4,632,026 bytes. From an existing Spanish image, **System language** produced English (`en-GB`) and an explicit **French** choice produced French; actual paste into TextEdit verified both translations, including `582741`. The source, English and French versions and both trace variants survived restarting the app.

Native shortcut testing on the private build 13 covered one region and two window captures with Photo / Balanced tracing. The pasted region and latest window results matched their saved SVG exactly, and the user confirmed the region capture opened no app window. **Native region/window capture and a fresh source-language capture were not repeated on build 14.** During the build-13 window test, macOS accepted the ready notification but suppressed its banner and sound because the display was being shared. Privacy settings were unchanged; visible and audible notification delivery is not claimed for this run.

These bounded observations do not establish every [acceptance criterion](https://github.com/colombod/smart-clipboard/blob/main/docs/UAT.md), all-provider coverage or complete accessibility support. This remains an opt-in preview, not a stable or fully accepted release.
